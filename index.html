Research Interests & Future Directions

These are proposed research directions reflecting my academic interests. They are not completed studies, ongoing projects, or published papers.

Proposed Research

Context-Aware Interfaces for Everyday Computing

Research Question

How can computing interfaces dynamically adapt to a user's current activity and environment to reduce cognitive load?

Motivation

Current interfaces treat every notification and display element equally regardless of context. Context-awareness could make technology less intrusive and more helpful.

Possible Methodology

User studies with prototype context-aware interfaces, comparing task completion and interruption metrics against conventional designs.

Future Scope

Integration with IoT environments, personalised adaptation models, and frameworks for context-aware UI design patterns.

Proposed Research

Privacy-Aware Smart Environments

Research Question

How can smart environments provide personalised services while giving users transparent, meaningful control over their data?

Motivation

As smart homes and workspaces collect more data, users need better mechanisms to understand and control what is captured and how it is used.

Possible Methodology

Design and evaluate privacy-control interfaces through participatory design sessions and controlled user experiments.

Future Scope

Privacy-by-design frameworks for ubiquitous computing, regulatory compliance tools, and user-facing data dashboards.

Proposed Research

Accessible Interfaces for Intelligent Assistants

Research Question

How can intelligent assistants be designed to be genuinely accessible to users with diverse abilities and technical backgrounds?

Motivation

Voice assistants and AI interfaces often assume a narrow range of user abilities. Inclusive design can broaden their benefit to more people.

Possible Methodology

Co-design workshops with diverse user groups, accessibility audits of existing assistants, and prototyping alternative interaction modalities.

Future Scope

Accessibility guidelines for conversational AI, adaptive interaction models, and inclusive design toolkits for AI systems.

Context-Aware Privacy Control System

A system that could dynamically adjust privacy settings in smart environments based on who is present, what activity is occurring, and user-defined comfort levels — providing granular, understandable data control.

Prior Art: Research pending — privacy-aware computing and contextual integrity literature to be reviewed.

A notification management concept where users set high-level preferences (e.g., "deep focus", "available", "winding down") and the system intelligently filters, delays, or bundles notifications accordingly.
