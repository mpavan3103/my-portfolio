PriceGuard AI

An automated market oversight dashboard designed to evaluate commodity price reasonability, detect artificial price gouging, and protect consumer welfare using statistical models.

Launch Live Demo View on GitHub
Domain Computational Pricing & Market HCI
Frontend Stack Vanilla JavaScript · Chart.js · CSS3

Verified System Disclosure

The public GitHub Pages demo runs browser-side calculations based on historical AGMARKNET and eNAM benchmark datasets. It demonstrates the interaction design, statistical bounds, and scoring logic. The standalone Python machine learning backend (Isolation Forest & anomaly pipelines) is documented and available in the GitHub repository.

01

Problem & Solution

The Challenge

Consumers and market supervisors often lack accessible, real-time mechanisms to evaluate whether retail commodity prices reflect legitimate market supply-demand fundamentals or unjustified retail inflation. Traditional agricultural market reports are dense, tabular, and difficult for non-specialists to interpret.

The Approach

PriceGuard AI structures complex price variance into an intuitive, accessible interface. By pairing dynamic statistical confidence bands with an ensemble anomaly score, the platform transforms raw market data into actionable clarity.

How It Works

PriceGuard AI combines a multi-stage statistical workflow with client-side interactive rendering.

STEP 01

Benchmark Ingestion

Historical price indices from eNAM and AGMARKNET reference benchmarks establish a robust statistical baseline.

STEP 02

Statistical Bounds

Computes mean price bands, coefficient of variation (CV), and rolling standard deviations for fair-value boundaries.

STEP 03

Anomaly Scoring

Z-score variance and Isolation Forest heuristics flag sudden price spikes that diverge significantly from normal seasonal trends.

STEP 04

Interface Rendering

Client-side Chart.js visualizations and reactive controls present intuitive comparisons without latency.

04

Key Implemented Features

  • ◆
    Reasonability Index Calculates a percentage score reflecting whether a commodity is priced fairly within computed statistical bounds.
  • ◆
    Side-by-Side Comparison Directly contrast two commodities across current price, volatility, trend percentage, and anomaly flags.
  • ◆
    12-Month Trend Visualisation Interactive historical curves rendered with Chart.js to inspect price movements across past quarters.
  • ◆
    Anomaly Alert Indicators Clear color-coded badges (Watch vs. Alert) reflecting Isolation Forest and standard deviation thresholds.
  • ◆
    Commodity Categories Instant filtering across staple groups: Grains, Vegetables, Dairy, and Daily Essentials.
  • ◆
    Responsive Dark Theme Engineered for seamless viewing across desktop monitors, tablets, and mobile devices.
